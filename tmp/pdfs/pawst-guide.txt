ABSTRACT 
Title of research: Pawst IT: A Micro-Social Media Pet Application with 
Integration of Gemini AI 
 
Proponents:  John Quenie S. Alceso 
RJ Anuel Louis C. Canlas 
Kherwyn Javee I. Sanchez 
Angeles III C. Tablante 
 
Degree:  Bachelor of Science in Information Technology 
 
Date of Completion: November 2024 
 
Keywords:  Gemini AI, Chatbot, Breed Identifier, Clinic Locator, Booking 
Appointment, React Native, cross-platform 
 
 
Pawst IT is a micro-social media platform created to meet the unique needs of pet owners 
and veterinarians, offering a dedicated space for accessing reliable pet care information, 
and simplifying access to veterinary resources . Pawst IT integrated Gemini AI to offer 
features such as a chatbot that answers general pet care and veterinary queries; and provides 
a breed identification feature through camera input and images which enhance user support 
with AI-driven insights. Key functionalities such as the Clinic Locator and Appointment 
Booking empower pet owners to locate and schedule visits with verified veterinary clinics, 
thereby promoting organized and accessible pet healthcare. Developed using Agile 
methodology, the project incorporates feedback from initial testing, refining usability and 
gathering ideas for potential future features. Pawst IT  also aligns with Angkop NGO's 
mission by supporting the spread of accurate pet care information, aiding both pet owners 
and the broader community in making informed decisions about pet welfare. Although  
there are areas for future enhancement, Pawst IT has established a robust foundation for 
facilitating pet care and fostering responsible pet ownership within an engaged community.

INTRODUCTION 
 
In today's digital age, social media platforms have become a popular way of 
communicating and interacting with various communities, including pet owners and 
veterinary professionals. However, this transition to digital spaces brings both benefits and 
challenges. Since people in this generation are dependent in the online world, pet 
community tends to gather information online rather than communicating with 
veterinarians directly; often relying on social media platforms for advice and support. The 
shift towards online information-gathering can lead to potential negative impacts, such as 
the risk of misinformation since most of information in the social media world is not 
verified. This creates a situation where misinformation spreads really fast that leads to  
numerous adverse comments about pets made by people who do not own them or are spam 
accounts. These problems weaken the trust among pet owners, veterinarians, and the 
general pet community because of flawed credibility and reliability of shared informatio n 
on these platforms. 
Cloud technology has revolutionized internet operations, empowering platforms like 
Facebook, Twitter, and TikTok to efficiently handle large volumes of user -generated 
content and facilitate real -time interactions . Pawst IT  will use cl oud technology, 
specifically Firebase for its real -time database, authentication, and cloud storage services 
that is crucial for enhancing users’ experience, secure authentication, and efficient data 
management. Additionally, Pawst IT will integrate artificial intelligence -powered image 
recognition for a breed identifier feature . This tool will enable users to snap pet photos 
within the app, instantly identifying the breed and accessing relevant information. Lastly, 
the proponents have chosen React Native  to develop the application, ensuring cross -
platform availability across iOS, Android, and  React JS for the web-based version of the 
application. This strategic decision ensures broad accessibility, allowing users to engage 
with Pawst IT regardless of their preferred device or operating system. 
The developers proposed the “ Pawst IT : A Micro -Social Media Pet Application  with 
Integration of Gemini AI”, which is a dedicated social media platform for pet owners and 
the veterinary community  that aims to bridge the gap in the digital pet  community by

Project Context 
The proposed application titled " Pawst IT: A Micro -Social Media Pet Application  with 
Integration of Gemini AI ," emerged from the existing problems of  the general pet 
community such as  the pet owners , the veterinarian community , and pet advocacy 
organizations. The proponents conducted interviews and surve y within the general pet 
community in Metro Manila, Philippines, including representatives from a local veterinary 
clinic, a pet influencer, a representative from a pet advocacy organization, and pet owners. 
In the interviews conducted with the general pet community, including Dr. Amanda Robles 
from Petlink 10th Avenue in Caloocan City, a pet influencer known as Chopper's Pawmily, 
and Mr. John Louis Gonzalez from ANGKOP PET NGO, common concerns emerged 
regarding the negative impact of social media on the pet community. The interviewees 
highlighted issues such as negative comments, online shaming, the proliferatio n of fake 
accounts, and the spread of false information about pets. Mr. Gonzalez specifically 
emphasized the challenge of building trust between shelters and adopters, suggesting  a 
more secured platform for the pet community. 
Additionally, the proponents c onducted a survey involving both Pet Owners and Pet 
Enthusiasts, with 350 respondents. The findings revealed significant challenges faced by 
this group. Notably, 65% of the respondents encountered difficulties in finding reliable pet 
care information. Furthermore, 87% expressed difficulty in selecting the right veterinarian.

General Problem of the Study 
How to develop “ Pawst IT : A Micro -Social Media Pet Application with 
Integration of Gemini AI”? 
The general pet community faces challenges in finding reliable pet care information 
due to information overload, misinformation, and fake accounts. Existing social 
media platforms offer generic features and lack dedicated tools for pet owners and 
pet enthusiasts, such as those powered by Gemini AI for breed identification and 
accessing breed -specific information. The security of existing social media 
platforms for pet-related interactions is often unsatisfactory, impacting the quality 
of engagement and information exchange within the general pet community. This 
can affect the relationship between owners and their pets, as misinformation and 
lack of trust can lead to misunderstandings and negative impacts on the human-pet 
relationship.

Scope and Limitations 
Scope: 
The "Pawst IT" application is designed to create a secure and interactive environ ment for 
pet owners, veterinarians, and administrators. It aims to facilitate community engagement, 
promote pet welfare, and provide essential services related to pet care. The following 
conditions outline the specific capabilities and limitations for each user category within the 
platform, ensuring clarity in roles and responsibilities. 
• Non-Verified Users can do the following:  
o Registration: Users can create an account on the platform. 
o Verification: Users can apply for verification by submitting necessary 
documentation, such as a government-issued ID and a clear facial photo 
for identity confirmation. 
o View Content: Users can browse content in all modules, including the 
Feed, Facts, and Adopt and Missing, without engaging or interacting. 
• Verified Users (Pet Owners) can do the following: 
o Engage with Content: Users can like, comment, and share posts. 
o Access Educational Content: Users can view educational materials 
shared in the Feed and Facts Module. 
o Post Content: Users can share moments with their pets in Feed Module.

Limitations: 
 
1. Focus on Pet-related Content 
Pawst IT  primarily focuses on pet -related content and interactions, potentially 
limiting its appeal to users seeking broader social media experiences. 
2. Limited Interactivity 
Pawst IT may lack certain interactive features found in mainstream social media 
platforms, such as live streaming, virtual events, which could affect user 
engagement and retention. 
3. Offline Functionality 
Pawst IT relies heavily on internet connectivity for most features, which may pose 

--- PDF PAGE 21 ---

challenges for users in areas with limited or no network coverage. 
4. Language Support 
Pawst IT  may have limitations in language support, which could hinder its 
accessibility and user engagement among non-English-speaking users. 
5. Regional Support 
Only mobile numbers registered in the Philippines will be able to verify t heir 
accounts on Pawst IT. 
6. Desktop Application 
The system is limited to web-based app, and not a stand-alone desktop application. 
7. Monetization Exclusion 
Pawst IT  does not include features for monetization, such as advertising, paid 
subscription, and any form of revenue generation through content creation. 
8. Limited Clinic Locator Functionality 
Pawst IT can only locate nearby clinics within a five-kilometer radius from the 
user's current location, potentially limiting access to veterinary services for users in 
areas with fewer clinics.

Development  
The development phase of Pawst IT  involved a structured approach to ensure the 
application met the desired functionalities, performance, and security requirements. The 
proponents followed established standards and best practices to create a strong and user-
friendly micro-social media platform for pet owners and veterinarians. 
1. Development of Standards and Practices 
a. Coding Standards 
• Consistency and Clarity:  Developers adhered to coding conventions 
for languages used (e.g., JavaScript for React  JS &  Native, and 
Firebase). This included naming conventions, file organization, and 
commenting practices to enhance code readability and maintainability. 
• Version Control:  The team used Git for version control, allowing 
multiple developers to collaborate efficiently. Each  feature was 
developed in separate branches, enabling code reviews and minimizing 
conflicts. 
b. Frameworks and Libraries 
• React Native: The proponents chose React Native for cross -platform 
mobile app development, allowing for shared codebases between iOS 
and Android while providing a native-like experience. 
• React JS: The proponents chose React JS for developing the web 
version of the application allowing a good experience for users. 
• Firebase: Integrated Firebase services for authentication, database 
management (Firestore), cloud storage, and analytics, streamlining 
backend processes and enhancing security.

leveraging feedback obtained from surveys with potential users, including pet owners and 
veterinarians. The testing process emphasized key quality characteristics defined by ISO 
25010, such as: 
• Functional Suitability : The effectiveness of core features, including breed 
identification, appointment booking, and chatbot responsiveness, was assessed to 
ensure they meet user needs and expectations. 
• Usability: User feedback highlighted areas f or improvement in navigation and 
overall user experience, requiring adjustments in intuitiveness and accessibility. 
• Performance Efficiency : Response times of the chatbot and loading speeds of 
various features were evaluated to ensure the app functions effi ciently under 
expected workloads. 
• Reliability: The stability and consistency of core functionalities were tested to 
confirm that users can rely on the app for accurate information and responsive 
operations. 
• Maintainability: Insights from UAT have informed design adjustments, 
facilitating easier updates and enhancements based on user needs.

Implementation Results 
This section presents insights from User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and the initial 
deployment of Pawst IT, evaluating the effectiveness of the system's functionalities and 
identifying key areas for improvement. Feedback gathered through UAT and interviews 
with key personnel  (Mr. John Louis Gonzales, representative of Angkop NGO; Ms. 
Christine Shane Calimlim, Clinic Administrator at Petlink; and Christine Gomez, 
Supervisor at Petlink) provides valuable direction for refining the app to ensure an 
optimized user experience. Below are the main findings: 
1. Successful System Deployment 
Pawst IT was successfully deployed on the web via Vercel and on Android through 
an APK accessible on the web. Testing on iOS devices was facilitated using Expo 
Go, as app store deployment requires additional certification. Core 
functionalities—including user account creation, identity verification, and features 
like the integration of Gemini AI (Chatbot and Breed Identifier), Clinic Locator, 
and Appointment Booking —operated smoothly across these platforms. Users 
responded positively to the app’s accessibility, ease  of use, and consistent feature 
availability across multiple devices. 
2. User Registration and Verification Outcomes 
The initial registration process was well -received, with users praising its ease of 
use. However, the manual verification process, managed by admins, highlighted 
opportunities for improvement, particularly in processing speed.  

--- PDF PAGE 109 ---

3. Feedback from Beta Testing 
Feedback during beta testing revealed that users found the interface intuitive and 
easy to navigate, reflected in high ease -of-use scores. However, some users 
indicated that additional improvements in error handling and responsiveness would 
enhance their experience, particularly in cases where bugs occur. Users appreciated 
having the bug report feature but expressed a desire for more detailed, immediate 
feedback on issue status after submission. These enhancements will be considered 
for future updates, allowing users to continue using the app with minimal disruption 
even when encountering minor issues, ultimately ensuring a better experience. 
4. Performance Under Load 
The system maintained consistent performance during average usage. However, 
stress testing showed minor response delays under high traffic, signaling a need for 
back-end optimizations. To address these issues, scalability improvements will be 
prioritized, ensuring the app remains responsive even during peak usage. 
5. Initial User Engagement and Content Moderation 
User engagement was steady across interactive modules like the Feed and 
Adoption/Missing sections. Manual content moderation, managed by admins, 
effectively maintained community standards. However, exploring automated 
moderation tools could help reduce the manual workload on administrators, 
ensuring faster response times and efficient content management. 
6. Insights for Future Development 
Based on the UAT results and user feedback, several areas for improvement were 
identified, particularly focusing on aspects that received an "Agree" rating, which 
suggests potential for enhancement. These include: 
• Error Handling : Implementing stronger error-handling and validation  
mechanisms to ensure smoother user experience when issue s arise. Improving 
the clarity of error messages and providing more user-friendly troubleshooting 
steps will help users resolve common issues independently.

CONCLUSION 
 
Summary 
The implementation of Pawst IT as part of this capstone project has been successful in 
meeting its goals. The app was deployed on web and Android platforms, with users able to 
create accounts, verify their identities, and access key features such as Gemini AI (chatbot 
and breed identifier), Clinic Locator, and Appointment Booking. Although the app has not 
yet been deployed on the iOS App Store, it was successfully tested on iOS devices via 
Expo Go. The app received positive feedback from users, who praised its intuitive interface 
and smooth functionality. However, several areas for improvement were identified, such 
as error handling, performance under high traffic, and adding more advanced search filters. 
These insights will inform future updates to enhance user satisfaction and app 
effectiveness.  
 
Conclusions 
1. Effective Implementation of Core Features : The core functionalities, including 
user registration, identity verification, Gemini AI, Clinic Locator, and Appointment 
Booking, were successfully implemented. These features address critical needs for 
pet owners, veterinarians, and Angkop NGO. Pet owners now have easy access to 
reliable pet care information and can conveniently book appointments with verified 
clinics. Veterinarians benefit from the appointment booking system, which 
streamlines their scheduling process and reduces administrative tasks. The platform 
also provides them with a dedicated space to directly engage with pet owners, 
allowing them to share valuable pet care information, promote their services, and 
raise awareness ab out important pet health issues. This functionality enables 
veterinarians to build stronger relationships with their clients, while also helping 
Angkop NGO expand their reach and promote responsible pet ownership 
initiatives. 
2. Positive Reception and Areas f or Enhancement : While the app was well -
received, beta testers provided valuable feedback that highlighted the need for 
improvements in error handling and additional search functionalities in the Clinic

pet owners and veterinarians and ensure that the platform continues to meet the 
evolving needs of the community. 
3. System Performance and Scalability : The app performed well under normal 
conditions, but stress testing revealed so me delays under high traffic. This points 
to the need for back -end optimization to improve scalability and performance, 
ensuring the app can handle increased demand. This is particularly important for 
veterinarians who rely on timely access to appointment schedules, and for Angkop 
NGO, which depends on smooth communication and reach to a growing user base. 
4. Addressing Challenges of Pet Owners, Veterinarians, and Angkop NGO : 
Pawst IT  has effectively addressed key challenges faced by pet owners, 
veterinarians, and Angkop NGO. For pet owners, the app provides easy access to 
accurate pet care information, a streamlined process for booking appointments, and 
personalized advice through Gemini AI. Veterinarians benefit from the 
appointment booking system and reduced administrative workload, allowing them 
to focus more on patient care. For Angkop NGO, the app offers a platform to 
educate and raise awareness about responsible pet ownership, vaccination, and 
other pet welfare initiatives, reaching a wider audience and e nhancing their 
advocacy efforts.

users.
